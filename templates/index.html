Domain-Specific Indian Political Sarcasm Rules

The 8 novel rules engineered for Indian political Twitter discourse that correct literal transformer misclassifications

8 Detection Rules + News Guard
⚡ Live Rule Simulator: Type any word or phrase to see which rule catches it
1
Explicit Sarcasm Hashtags
100% Precision
Matches overt sarcasm and irony tags: #sarcasm, #irony, #justsaying, #notreally. Authors explicitly declare non-literal intent.
#sarcasm #irony #justsaying
2
Punctuation Overload
1,436 Matches
Detects exaggerated punctuation: !!, ???, !?!, ..... Frequently used in sarcastic rhetorical disbelief in political disputes.
!!+ \?{2,} !\?+
3
Indian Political Slang Lexicon
1,254 Matches
Domain vocabulary: andhbhakt, jumla, feku, pappu, godi media, chamcha, khata khat, nepotism. Inherently satirical or derogatory terms unique to Indian political Twitter.
andhbhakt jumla feku godi media
4
Sarcastic Emoji Signals
235 Matches
Emoji signals strongly correlated with mockery: 🤡 (clown), 🙄 (eye-roll), 😏 (smirk), 🤦 (facepalm). Inverts surface positive sentiment into ridicule.
🤡 clown 🙄 eye roll 😏 smirk
5
"So-Called" / Dismissive Syntax
235 Matches
Syntactic patterns using "so called", "self proclaimed", "supposedly" to delegitimize political opponents or claimed achievements.
so called self claimed supposedly
6
EVM & Election Fraud Rhetoric
Accusatory
Detects fixed-election skepticism: "evm tampering", "vote chori", "match fixing", "democracy murdered". Disguised mockery of electoral fairness.
evm tampering vote chori
7
Rhetorical Degradation Patterns
Critical
Rhetorical questions followed by degradation: "Modi what's he? divider in chief", "Rahul what has he done?". High negative polarity masked as inquiry.
divider in chief what has he done
8
Ironic Compliment Structure
High Impact
Mock praise structure: pairing grand superlative words ("masterstroke", "sanskaar", "visionary") with negative outcomes (inflation, train delays, scandals).
masterstroke sanskaar for BJP
